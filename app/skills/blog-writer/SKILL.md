# Blog Writing Instructions

## Step 1: Understand the Brief
Before writing, clarify:
- **Topic**: What are we writing about?
- **Audience**: Who is reading? (developers, executives, consumers)
- **Goal**: What should the reader do after reading?
- **Tone**: Professional, conversational, technical, or thought-leadership?
- **Length**: Short (400-600), standard (800-1200), or long-form (1500-2500)

## Step 2: Structure the Post
Use `load_skill_resource` to read `references/blog-templates.md` for templates.

### Standard Structure
1. **Title** (50-60 chars): Primary keyword + benefit/curiosity
2. **Hook** (2-3 sentences): Question, stat, or bold statement
3. **Context** (1-2 paragraphs): Why this matters now
4. **Body** (3-5 sections with H2 headings): Core content
5. **Takeaways** (bullet list): 3-5 actionable items
6. **CTA** (1 paragraph): What to do next

## Step 3: Writing Rules
- Paragraphs: 2-3 sentences maximum
- Use bullet lists every 3-4 paragraphs
- Include data: numbers, percentages, named sources
- Active voice always ("We analyzed" not "An analysis was conducted")
- No jargon without definition
- Link to sources and internal content

## Step 4: SEO Essentials
- Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, and 2-3 H2s
- Meta description: 150-160 chars with CTA
- Alt text on all images
- Internal links: 2-3 per post
- External links: 3-5 to authoritative sources
