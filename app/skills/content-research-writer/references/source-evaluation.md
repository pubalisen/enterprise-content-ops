# Source Evaluation Framework

## The CRAAP Test
Evaluate every source against these criteria:

### Currency
- When was the information published or last updated?
- Is it current enough for your topic?
- Are links functional?

### Relevance
- Does it relate directly to your thesis?
- Is the depth appropriate for your audience?
- Would you cite this in a professional setting?

### Authority
- Who is the author? What are their credentials?
- Is the publisher reputable?
- Is there contact information or institutional affiliation?

### Accuracy
- Is the information supported by evidence?
- Has it been peer-reviewed or vetted?
- Can you verify claims through other sources?
- Are there spelling/grammar errors (quality signal)?

### Purpose
- Why does this source exist? (inform, persuade, sell, entertain)
- Is the point of view objective or biased?
- Are there political, ideological, or commercial motivations?

## Source Scoring
- **A-tier** (5/5 CRAAP): Primary research, peer-reviewed, named expert
- **B-tier** (3-4/5): Reputable journalism, industry reports
- **C-tier** (2/5): Blog posts, opinion pieces (use only for context)
- **Reject** (0-1/5): Anonymous, outdated, biased, unverifiable

## Minimum Standards
- Every article must have at least 3 A-tier sources
- No claim should rely solely on a C-tier source
- Include publication year for all citations
